Goals

The aim of this course is to introduce students with the modern methods of mechanics applied to biological systems.

Content

Methods of vector algebra and calculus in their vector forms as well as the indicial form. The Kroenecker and permutation operators. Concepts relating to the deformation and motion of continuum media. Introduction of the concept of mass, force, surface traction and stress. Derivation of the fundamental principles of conservation.

Contribution of the Course to Engineering Education

Opens a pathway for the student to pursue further studies in tissue biomechanics, by laying a solid foundation of mechanical concepts and principles.
