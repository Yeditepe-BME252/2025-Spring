* Why does this course exist?
The *first course in Biomechanics* offered by the Biomedical Engineering Department aims to enhance the understanding of the science of motion and mechanical interactions between living tissue.
#+ATTR_REVEAL: :frag (appear)
*Essential reading*: Chapter 1 of [[IB:SFAD/15][IB:SFAD/15]]
#+ATTR_REVEAL: :frag (appear appear appear ...)
- Point of Departure, the development of biomechanics
- Health Care Applications, complex mechanical phenomenon in living organisms
- What Is Continuum Mechanics?
- A Brief on Cell Biology, 
- The Extracellular Matrix
- Mechanotransduction in Cells
  
* Syllabus
** Course learning objectives-CLOs
#+ATTR_REVEAL: :frag (appear appear appear ...)
1. [ ] exemplify some basic biomechanical phenomenon that arise in living tissues ()
2. [ ] perform simple algebraic operations with vectors
3. [ ] read and verify expressions written in the indicial notation
4. [ ] convert expressions between their tensor and indicial forms
5. [ ] transform vectors linearly via second-order tensors
6. [ ] convert expressions between their tensor and matrix forms
7. [ ] perform skew-symmetric decomposition of a second-order tensor
8. [ ] perform change of basis of vector components
9. [ ] calculate the determinant of a second-order tensor
10. [ ] calculate the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of a second-order tensor
11. [ ] provide examples of scalar and vector fields
12. [ ] apply the gradient operator to  a scalar field
13. [ ] apply the gradient operator to a vector field
14. [ ] identify divergence, curl and laplacian operators in cartesian coordinates
15. [ ] differentiate between formulations using cartesian and curvilinear coordinates
#+REVEAL: split
#+ATTR_HTML: :start 16
#+ATTR_REVEAL: :frag (appear appear appear ...)
16. [ ] apply the divergence theorem to integral forms
17. [ ] use density to find continuum mass and center of mass
18. [ ] differentiate surface and body forces
19. [ ] provide an example to the surface traction vector
20. [ ] relate the surface traction vector and the stress tensor
21. [ ] write the condition of static equilibrium in global form
22. [ ] obtain the local form of static equilibrium using the divergence theorem

31. [ ] solve for a mechanical variable using the balance laws

* Code of Conduct & Ethics
#+ATTR_REVEAL: :frag (appear appear appear ...)
- The interpersonal communication in class is based on a level of minimum respect towards each other.
- The class is a communal space, it is not a stage to show off (except for the instructor).
- Students are expected to arrive to class on a voluntary basis, involuntary participation is strongly discouraged.
- Mechanics requires a direct teaching model where the role of the student is usually limited to a receiver, however, the students are encouraged to ask questions and participate in the learning process.
- Here’s a fascinating account of ethics (in Turkish): [[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q88NhXSmK_I][Etik Değerler ve Eğitimi]] by Prof. Ioanna Kuçuradi (Maltepe University).
