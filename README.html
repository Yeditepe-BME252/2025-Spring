Course learning objectives (CLOs)

  1. [X] to use basic vector algebra operations in biomechanics
  2. [X] to identify the forces appearing as a result of body isolation
  3. [X] to determine the center of gravity of single or connected bodies
  4. [X] to simplify a system of forces into single force-moment pair
  5. [X] to convert the principles of equilibrium into equations
  6. [X] to use algebraic manipulations in solving linear equation(s)
  7. [X] to solve statics of basic human articulations
  8. [X] to apply conversions between internal forces and stresses
  9. [ ] to use simple constitutive relations between stress and strain
  10. [ ] to have basic knowledge about material properties of biological tissues
